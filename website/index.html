What it does

A whole type studio,
in one small app.

  • Morph fonts

    Blend any two typefaces curve to curve. Letters stay on the baseline and x-height.

  • Motion timeline

    Entrances, loops, paths of stops and letter-by-letter text, edited on a timeline.

  • Live materials

    Chrome, glass, neon, holo, plush, riso and pixel, drawn by GPU shaders in real time.

  • Pen and shapes

    A pen that snaps where you mean it, plus a kit of shapes and stackable modifiers.

  • Real fonts out

    Compile your lettering into an installable TTF, WOFF or a web font kit.

  • Kerning editor

    Drag letter pairs into place, with classes and exceptions written to GPOS.

  • Trace and pixel

    Trace pictures into vector marks, or turn them into pixel art with dithering.

  • Export anything

    PNG, JPG, SVG, MP4 and WebM, exported at full quality and the same every time.

What’s new

Version 0.9.1

  • A motion editor. A timeline dock with a row for every moving layer: drag to retime, click to edit.
  • A path of stops. Then › Move to… goes on through any number of stops, each with its own wait and feel.
  • Letter by letter. Text moves one letter or word at a time, without breaking it apart.
  • A kerning editor. Drag pairs into place, with classes, suggestions and exceptions.
  • Smoother morphs. Blended fonts are drawn curve to curve, with a third of the points.
  • Faster everywhere. Home loads half the code, and moving shapes no longer stall the GPU.
